We're are building Sokkel to creating building digital products feel the way it
should, smooth, clear and almost invisible. Today we're a small team in beta,
onboarding our first pilots, but we have bigger ideas about where this goes. We
need the right people to help us get there.

We believe the only real unfair advantage a startup can build is a brand, not
just the logo, typography, colours, but the whole feeling someone has when they
encounter you. Every word, every interaction, every moment of clarity or
confusion. We want to find someone who thinks about this the way we do.


## We hope you

We hope that you have strong opinions about language and know how to defend
them, love finding the exact right word, not just a good enough one. That you
can zoom out to strategy and zoom in to a single sentence. Read widely, steal
ideas shamelessly, and bring unexpected references into your work. Potentially
the most important, are genuinely curious about business, technology, and
people. 

And importantly: you don't see a hard line between brand and business. When
there's an event to run, a sales conversation to support, or a campaign to get
out the door; you're in. You understand that in a small team, the best work
happens when you're close to the customer.

We would love if you have experience in brand, communication, or content from
startup, technology company or agency. Been involved in go-to-market, events,
or sales enablement in some capacity.

## Our offer

We offer a competitive salary, a real role where you shape Sokkel and a part of
a team that is building something we believe in.
